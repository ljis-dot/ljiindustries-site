FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to be technical to work with you?

No. Sessions are in plain English, and everything we set up comes with simple instructions and training for your team.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Small businesses and solo operators across industries: service businesses, professional practices, retail, trades, nonprofits, and more. If your team spends time on repetitive admin, there's usually something worth automating.

What happens in the Starter Session?

We spend 60 minutes reviewing your workflows and tools, identify 2–3 realistic quick wins, and flag any risks. You'll receive a short written action plan afterward.

Is my business data safe?

Privacy is part of every recommendation. We favor tools with clear data-protection terms, avoid putting sensitive data where it doesn't belong, and can help you write an AI use policy for your team.

Which AI tools do you use?

Whatever fits your business and budget. Often that means getting more out of tools you already pay for before adding anything new. Recommendations are vendor-neutral.
