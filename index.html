FAQ

Common questions

What is LJI Industries?

LJI Industries is a digital content studio. We publish Briefed, a daily news website, podcast, and short news videos.

What is Briefed?

Briefed is a daily US-focused news briefing. Each story comes with the facts and links to original reporting and official sources, so you can see what's confirmed and what's still developing.

How do I listen to the podcast?

The Briefed podcast is a short daily episode covering the day's top US news in about two minutes. Subscribe with the RSS feed. It is also available on Spotify.

When is a new edition published?

A new Briefed edition is posted every morning at ljiindustries.com/briefed, around 4:45 AM Eastern Time.
