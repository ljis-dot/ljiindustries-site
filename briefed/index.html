DOJ charges James Comey a third time over a 2016 leak via criminal information

Unsealed Monday: DOJ charged former FBI Director James Comey on Oct. 5 by criminal information — not a grand jury indictment — with transmitting national defense information and conspiracy tied to an Oct. 2016 New York Times story. His lawyers say it is past the 10-year limit; two earlier U.S. attorney's offices declined. Comey called it "an appalling abuse of the Department of Justice." A prior case was dismissed; he was indicted in North Carolina April 28 over an '8647' post.

9th Circuit 2-1 won't block counting ~20,000 disputed California ballots

A Ninth Circuit panel, 2-1, dismissed the DOJ's emergency appeal seeking to set aside about 20,000 California mail ballots tied to people the government labeled likely noncitizens. Judges Bennett (Trump appointee) and Sanchez (Biden appointee) held that DOJ did not give the district court adequate time to rule; Judge Bumatay dissented. The order is not on the merits. A district hearing was set for today.
