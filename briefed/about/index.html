About Briefed

Why Briefed exists

I got tired of partisan news: slanted headlines, endless opinion, and spin on top of every story, when all I wanted was the facts.

Briefed strips out the extra opinion and gives you the facts on things that really happened.

Every story links to the original reporting and the official sources, so you can check it yourself.

The audio is AI-voiced from the written edition. AI-voiced

What changed recently

  • Oct 25, 2026: I switched Briefed to a US-focused edition. The lead story is always a domestic one, and world news only makes the cut when I can say why it matters here.
  • Oct 25, 2026: I added "In their own words": firsthand quotes from the people involved, each linked to its source.
  • Oct 25, 2026: I tightened the top of each edition. The date, the player and the story being read now stay pinned while you scroll, on phone and desktop.
  • Oct 25, 2026: Each edition now shows its edition number, and every story has a one-tap "Was this useful?" button so I can learn what to keep doing.
